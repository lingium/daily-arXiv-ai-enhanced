### [1] [Estimating Treatment Effects for Depression in Longitudinal Therapy Switching Settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27214)
*Xinyu Qin,Martin Katzman,Alexandria Greifenberger,Elssa Toumeh,Sachinthya Lokuge,Tia Sternat,Ruiheng Yu,Lu Wang*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 该研究使用纵向重度抑郁症（MDD）临床试验数据，构建了下一访视的反事实预测任务来估计不同治疗方案下的HAMD-17评分，并基准测试了8种估计器。因果森林（CF）表现最佳，研究发现症状获益集中在特定换药方向，剂量强化通常有益，但存在低强度方案优于高强度方案的反直觉例外。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 抑郁症治疗常因疗效不足或副作用需要换药，但在此场景下估计个体化治疗效果极具挑战：治疗分配受患者特征混杂影响、换药引入时变性选择偏差、随访数据中无法观测反事实结果。现有方法难以准确估计换药情境下的个体化治疗效果。

Method: 利用专有的纵向MDD临床试验数据，构建下一访视反事实预测任务，估计不同替代治疗下HAMD-17总分。基准测试8种估计器，包括元学习器（meta-learners）、基于残差的方法和基于树的方法。

Result: 因果森林（CF）在所有评估标准上表现最佳且最稳定。症状获益集中在特定换药方向，剂量强化通常有益。发现一个反直觉例外：对特定患者亚组，低强度方案优于高强度方案。粗观测比较会大幅高估获益，而混杂校正后的估计产生适度且可行动的效应量。

Conclusion: 研究发现提供了可前瞻性检验的临床决策支持候选方案，有助于改善抑郁症治疗中的换药决策。

Abstract: Depression treatment often requires switching medications due to inadequate response or adverse effects. Estimating individualized treatment effects in this setting is challenging because treatment assignment is confounded by patient characteristics, switching induces time-varying selection, and counterfactual outcomes are not observed in follow-up data. Using a proprietary longitudinal major depressive disorder (MDD) clinical trial dataset, we formulate a next-visit counterfactual prediction task to estimate Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD-17) total scores under alternative treatments. We benchmark 8 estimators, including meta-learners, residual-based methods, and tree-based approaches. Causal Forest (CF) demonstrates the most favorable and consistent performance across all criteria. Our analysis shows that symptom benefits concentrate in specific switch directions, with dose intensification being generally beneficial. Notably, we identify a counterintuitive exception where a lower-intensity regimen outperforms a higher-intensity alternative for specific patient subsets. While crude observational comparisons substantially overstate gains, confounding-adjusted estimates yield modest, actionable magnitudes. These findings provide prospectively testable candidates for clinical decision support in depression care.

### [2] [HOMER: Huber-of-Means for Efficient and Robust Estimation in Hilbert Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27532)
*Kisung You,Boram Cho*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文提出 HOMER（Huber-of-Means for Efficient and Robust Estimation），一种通过径向 Huber 中心聚合块均值的鲁棒估计方法。其形式和伪形式分别恢复样本均值的二次区域特性并随阈值增长趋近样本均值，在有限二阶矩下建立 Hilbert 空间多数定理与 MOM 阶偏差界，并在有限三阶矩下支持参数速率的均值推断。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统重尾分布下均值估计缺乏高置信控制，几何中位数（MOM）虽鲁棒但缺乏向均值效率移动的阈值机制，无法在鲁棒性和效率之间灵活插值。

Method: HOMER 通过径向 Huber 中心聚合块均值，提供规范形式（有界块分数，在二次区域内恢复样本均值）和伪形式（阈值增大时趋近样本均值），并建立 Hilbert 空间多数定理和 MOM 阶偏差界。

Result: 在有限三阶矩下，固定有限维投影支持参数速率的均值推断，需块大小和块数同步增长且块大小增速更快。仿真显示重尾数据下对少数块位移稳定，干净高斯数据下效率接近样本均值；但有限块 sandwich 区间在偏斜函数数据下欠覆盖，且当污染影响多数块或损害块内均值时方法失效。

Conclusion: HOMER 在中位数鲁棒性与均值效率之间提供可调插值，理论上支持参数速率推断，但在有限块情形和严重污染场景下仍存在局限性，需要谨慎使用或进一步改进。

Abstract: Heavy tails weaken high-confidence control for the empirical mean. Geometric median-of-means (MOM) also lacks a threshold that moves toward mean efficiency. We propose \emph{HOMER}, or Huber-of-Means for Efficient and Robust Estimation. HOMER aggregates block means through a radial Huber center. Its canonical and pseudo-Huber forms bound each block score and interpolate between median-like robustness and the empirical mean. We establish a Hilbert-space majority theorem and a MOM-order deviation bound under a finite second moment. Canonical HOMER recovers the sample mean inside its quadratic region. Pseudo-HOMER approaches the sample mean as the threshold grows. It also admits asymptotic linearity and consistent sandwich covariance estimation around the population block-Huber target. Under a finite third moment, fixed finite-dimensional projections support mean inference at the usual parametric rate. This result requires growing block sizes and counts, with block sizes increasing faster. Heavy-tailed simulations show that HOMER remains stable when a minority of block summaries is displaced. On clean Gaussian data, both versions closely approach the empirical mean's efficiency. Finite-block sandwich intervals undercovered, especially for skewed functional data. Further studies show failure when contamination affects most blocks or compromises ordinary within-block means.

### [3] [Robust Estimation of Sparse Numerical Vectors under Local Differential Privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27815)
*Puning Zhao,Zhikun Zhang,Shaowei Wang,Sheng Yue,Bangzhou Xin,Tianhang Zheng,Pengfei Zhang,Xiaochun Cao*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种名为RPC（Randomized Projection with Clipping）的方法，用于解决多物品用户在本地差分隐私下对投毒攻击的鲁棒稀疏向量均值估计问题。通过随机投影和裁剪，并对裁剪偏差进行精确修正，在消除偏差-方差权衡的同时增强了鲁棒性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的LDP防御策略主要针对单物品用户，而在实际场景中用户可能拥有多个物品。多物品用户面临更大的输出空间，使攻击者能够发动更强大且难以被检测的投毒攻击，因此亟需针对多物品用户的鲁棒防御方法。

Method: 提出RPC方法：(1) 服务器向每个用户发送随机二值向量，用户将本地数据投影到该向量上并裁剪值以限制攻击者的能力；(2) 通过精确的偏差表达式提出修正方法，从而无需再进行偏差-方差权衡；(3) 可以进一步降低裁剪阈值，缩小输出空间，增强鲁棒性。

Result: 提供了在所有可能攻击下的估计误差的严格理论保证。数值实验表明：在可信环境下，新方法与现有方法相比性能相当或更优；在不可信环境下，该方法对投毒攻击的鲁棒性显著更强。

Conclusion: RPC方法不仅本身是高效的估计器，而且对投毒攻击具有显著的鲁棒性，有效解决了多物品用户LDP场景下的投毒攻击防御难题。

Abstract: Local differential privacy (LDP) protocols are vulnerable to poisoning attacks. Existing research have proposed efficient defense strategies for single-item users. However, in practice, a user may possess multiple items. The defense against poisoning attacks for multi-item users is challenging, because due to larger output spaces, the adversary can conduct more powerful attacks without being detected. In this paper, we address the robust sparse vector mean estimation problem, in which each user has a vector with $m$ nonzero coordinates. We propose Randomized Projection with Clipping (RPC). Firstly, the server sends a random binary vector to each user. The user then projects its local data on the vector, and clip the value to restrict the attacker's capability. To handle clipping bias, we propose a correction method based on a careful analysis that gives an exact expression of the bias. As a result, bias-variance tradeoff is no longer needed, thus the clipping threshold can be further reduced to shrink the output space and enhance robustness. We provide a rigorous theoretical guarantee of the estimation error under all possible attacks. Numerical experiments show that under trusted environments, our new method achieves comparable or better performance than existing methods, indicating that our method is already an efficient estimator in its own right. Under untrusted environments, our method is also significantly more robust to poisoning attacks.

</details>
