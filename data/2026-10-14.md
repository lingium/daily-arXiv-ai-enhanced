### [1] [Scalable Learning of Determinantal Point Processes with Kernel-Structured Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12829)
*Takahiro Kawashima,Hideitsu Hino*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文提出一种可扩展的DPP学习方法，通过非线性核与随机特征避免低秩假设带来的表达力-计算权衡，提升泛化性能且参数高效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 有限集上的DPP用正定核矩阵编码项间相似性并体现排斥性。将DPP学习扩展到大数据集通常需对核矩阵施加结构约束（如低秩加对角形式），但这类结构假设会导致表达能力与计算可扩展性之间的严重权衡。

Method: (a) 用非线性核函数与潜在特征对满秩交互建模；(b) 为最大似然估计设计代理函数；(c) 采用非线性核的随机特征进行随机优化。

Result: 在真实数据集上的数值实验表明，本方法显著改善上述权衡，以出色的参数效率取得更好的泛化性能。

Conclusion: 通过非线性核建模、代理目标与随机特征优化，可在不依赖低秩结构假设的前提下实现可扩展且表达力强的DPP学习。

Abstract: Determinantal point processes (DPPs) on a finite set are probability models that exhibit repulsion where inter-item similarities are encoded by a positive definite kernel matrix. Scaling DPP learning to large datasets typically requires imposing structural constraints on the kernel matrix, such as a low-rank-plus-diagonal form. However, structural assumptions introduce a severe trade-off between expressive power and computational scalability. Our study addresses this challenge by (a) modeling the full-rank interactions via a nonlinear kernel function and latent features, (b) designing a surrogate function for the maximum likelihood estimator, and (c) adopting stochastic optimization using random features of the nonlinear kernel. Numerical experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves this trade-off, achieving better generalization performance with remarkable parameter efficiency.

### [2] [Universal Bounds for Self-Normalized Kullback-Leibler Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12851)
*Buu Phan,Lester Mackey,David Alvarez-Melis*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文研究基于归一化重要性权重的KL散度熵估计器，证明其期望至多超过目标-提议KL散度log 2，并将其应用于生成模型测试时对齐与相对熵编码。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 需要一种无需重要性权重有界或高阶矩有限假设的KL散度估计与误差界，以支持测试时对齐和编码等实际场景。

Method: 从归一化重要性权重构造熵基KL估计器；证明有限样本普遍界；刻画重要性加权重采样分布与提议分布之间的KL散度并给出上界。

Result: 估计器期望最多超过目标-提议KL散度log 2；重采样分布与提议分布的KL散度同时被估计器期望和目标-提议KL散度上界所控制，且无需权重有界或有限高阶矩。

Conclusion: 将该理论用于Soft Best-of-N测试时对齐与相对熵编码，得到有限样本策略散度证书和无需矩条件的编码代价界。

Abstract: This work studies an entropy-based estimator of Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence constructed from normalized importance weights. We prove a universal finite-sample bound showing that its expectation can exceed the target-proposal KL divergence by at most $\log 2$ for any sample size. We then characterize the KL divergence between the distribution induced by importance-weighted resampling and the proposal, showing that it is upper bounded by both the expectation of the entropy-based estimator and the target-proposal KL divergence. Importantly, these bounds require neither bounded importance weights nor finite higher moments. We apply these results to test-time alignment of generative models via Soft Best-of-$N$ sampling, an importance-sampling-based scaling technique proposed by Verdun et al. (2025), and to relative entropy coding, obtaining finite-sample policy-divergence certificates and moment-free bounds on coding cost.

</details>
