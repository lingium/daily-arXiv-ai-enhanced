### [1] [Agnostic Model-Assisted Estimation with Machine Learning for Survey Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10904)
*Ziming An,Mehdi Dagdoug,David Haziza,Yves Tillé*

Main category: stat.ME

TL;DR: 提出与学习器无关的模型辅助估计框架，用交叉拟合实现设计无偏、渐近正态与方差估计，并澄清设计感知/无关交叉拟合的条件。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有模型辅助估计中虽有灵活预测方法，但理论结果多针对特定方法，缺乏统一的分析框架。

Method: 用统一般条件替代逐个学习器的分析；连接设计感知与设计无关交叉拟合，刻画使折间条件独立的抽样设计；采用条件加权获得精确设计无偏性，建立与oracle估计的一阶等价。

Result: 得到设计一致性与渐近正态性；给出条件与原始纳入概率一阶一致的条件；基于交叉拟合残差构造一致方差估计与渐近有效置信区间；在额外正则条件下达到Godambe-Joshi下界，实现渐近最优；模拟显示交叉拟合显著降低有限样本偏差并改善方差估计与覆盖率。

Conclusion: 交叉拟合的模型辅助估计可在保留设计推断的同时获得渐近最优性质，且适用于多种自适应学习器。

Abstract: Model-assisted estimation uses prediction rules to improve the efficiency of estimators of finite population parameters while retaining design-based inference. Although flexible prediction methods have been considered, existing theoretical results are largely method-specific. We develop a learner-agnostic framework that replaces separate analyses for individual learners with general conditions on the sampling design and prediction error. We connect design-aware and design-agnostic cross-fitting and characterize the sampling designs under which they yield conditional independence across folds. Under suitable conditions, conditional weighting gives exact design-unbiasedness. We establish first-order equivalence to oracle estimators, leading to design consistency and asymptotic normality, and clarify when conditional and original inclusion probabilities yield the same first-order behavior. We propose consistent variance estimators based on cross-fitted residuals and construct asymptotically valid confidence intervals. Under additional model and regularity conditions, we establish asymptotic optimality through attainment of the Godambe--Joshi lower bound. Simulations show that cross-fitting substantially reduces finite-sample bias and improves variance estimation and coverage with adaptive learners.

### [2] [Bayesian Variable Selection for High-Dimensional Predictors with Missing Psychometric Outcomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11032)
*Zongyue Teng,Shujie Ma,Timothy J. Hohman,Angela L. Jefferson,Panpan Zhang*

Main category: stat.ME

TL;DR: 提出SHIM贝叶斯框架，结合分层马蹄收缩与缺失结果贝叶斯处理，实现结构化变量选择，兼顾预测层级、结果依赖与多变量结局缺失。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 心理测量研究中常见高维、多模态预测变量及部分观测的多变量结局，但现有正则化方法多不适用：既难处理预测变量的层级结构，又主要面向单变量结局。

Method: SHIM将分层马蹄（horseshoe）收缩先验与缺失结局的贝叶斯处理相结合，联合建模预测变量层级、结局间依赖及不完整多变量响应；建立所提先验的理论性质，并通过模拟研究进行评估。

Result: 模拟显示SHIM在灵敏度与假阳性控制间取得平衡，系数估计准确且不确定性量化良好；应用于阿尔茨海默病队列，刻画多模态神经影像与多变量神经心理结局的关联，并生成基于后验的多重插补用于分析体液生物标志物与认知的关系。

Conclusion: SHIM为高维层级预测变量与不完整多变量结局提供了统一的贝叶斯结构化变量选择方案，具理论保证与实证有效性，并公开R包shim便于应用。

Abstract: High-dimensional, multimodal predictors and partially observed multivariate outcomes are common in psychometric research. However, existing regularization methods often do not accommodate hierarchical predictor structures and are primarily designed for univariate outcomes. We propose SHIM, a Bayesian framework for structured variable selection that combines hierarchical horseshoe shrinkage with a Bayesian treatment of missing outcomes. The framework jointly accommodates predictor hierarchies, dependence among outcomes, and incomplete multivariate responses. We establish theoretical properties of the proposed prior specification and evaluate SHIM through simulation studies. The results demonstrate that SHIM balances sensitivity with false-positive control while yielding accurate coefficient estimates and well-calibrated uncertainty quantification. We further apply SHIM to data from an Alzheimer's disease cohort to characterize associations between multimodal neuroimaging measures and multivariate neuropsychological outcomes and to generate posterior-based multiple imputations for downstream analyses of the relationships between fluid biomarkers and cognition. An R package, shim, is publicly available to facilitate implementation.

### [3] [Risk-Averse Decision Making with Multi-Level Reliability Guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11524)
*Amirmohammad Farzaneh,Osvaldo Simeone*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 研究在系统状态不确定下最大化多目标中断水平证书的加权平均，等价于嵌套预测集优化，并给出可解对偶形式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多工程应用（如无线广播）需要针对不同目标中断水平提供性能证书，且需在真实系统状态不确定的情况下做出稳健决策。

Method: 将问题转化为嵌套预测集上的优化，与conformal prediction文献建立联系，并推广单级风险规避决策方法；推导对偶形式以解耦不同输入值上的优化。

Result: 在基于分集的无线传输系统上进行数值实验，展示了用单一共享策略满足多级证书的代价，并刻画多个可靠性水平间的Pareto权衡。

Conclusion: 多级性能证书问题可统一为嵌套预测集优化框架，对偶分解提供计算途径，实验揭示共享策略在多可靠性目标下的权衡成本。

Abstract: Many applications in engineering, including wireless broadcasting, require designs that provide performance certificates at different target outage levels. This paper studies the problem of maximizing the weighted average of such certificates in the presence of uncertainty about the true system state. The problem is shown to be equivalent to an optimization over nested prediction sets, connecting to the literature on conformal prediction and extending prior art on single-level risk-averse decision making. Furthermore, we derive a dual formulation that decouples optimization across input values. Numerical experiments on a diversity-based wireless transmission system illustrate the cost of enforcing multi-level certificates with a single shared policy and trace the Pareto trade-off between multiple reliability levels.

### [4] [Generative Marketing Mix Modeling: A Causal Inference Framework Linking GEO and GEM to Business Impact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11915)
*Masahiro Kato,Daiki Honma,Taka Kato*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 提出生成式营销组合建模(GMMM)，用于估计GEO和GEM的因果效应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成式AI改变了企业与客户的触达方式，但标准营销数据无法记录用户在生成答案中看到并注意到企业名称的频率，因此缺乏衡量GEO和GEM效果的方法。

Method: GMMM框架：对GEO，结合重复生成的答案、问题计数、跨生成系统的使用份额及注意概率；对GEM，结合付费植入记录与注意概率。通过比较不同处理序列下的期望业务响应，并给出识别因果效应的充分条件。

Result: 使用英语和日语的产品推荐模拟答案，对所提方法的实证表现进行了考察。

Conclusion: GMMM为生成式引擎环境下的营销效果测量提供了因果识别框架，并有待模拟数据验证其表现。

Abstract: Generative artificial intelligence changes how firms reach customers, but standard marketing data do not record how often users see and notice a firm's name in generated answers. We develop Generative Marketing Mix Modeling (GMMM) to estimate the causal effects of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Generative Engine Marketing (GEM). For GEO, GMMM combines repeated generated answers with question counts, shares of use across generative systems, and notice probabilities. For GEM, it combines records of sponsored placements with notice probabilities. GMMM compares expected business responses under alternative treatment sequences and establishes sufficient conditions for identifying the resulting effects. We investigate the empirical performance of the proposed method using simulated answers to product recommendation in English and Japanese.

</details>
