### [1] [The Elliptically Optimal Confidence Interval: A Bivariate Extension of Wilson's Score Method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10865)
*Nawaf Mohammed*

Main category: math.ST

TL;DR: 提出 Elliptically Optimal (EO) 置信区间，用于两个独立二项比例之差 p1−p2，通过投影联合正态近似的椭圆区域并闭式求解其极值，得到六种情形下的显式界限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 两独立二项比例之差的置信区间存在 nuisance 方向未被估计量识别的问题。单样本 Wilson score 区间无法直接推广到双变量情形，因为反转联合正态近似得到的是单位正方形内的椭圆区域，而 p1−p2 是一维的。

Method: 将 EO 区间定义为椭圆区域内 p1−p2 的取值范围，闭式求解该优化问题，得到六种互斥且完备情形下的显式界限。该区间等价于对 nuisance 方差取最大化（而非估计）得到的 score 区间，是投影椭圆区域所得的最短区间。推导精确覆盖超额 2[Φ(zR)−Φ(z)]，其中 R 为最不利标准差与真实标准差之比；并用精确枚举检验覆盖。

Result: EO 区间在正态近似下从不高估不足（never under-covers），界始终可允许且非退化，但两比例都极端时会产生过度覆盖，并精确量化。覆盖超额在参数空间一条显式直线上消失，上界为 α，且在样本量等比例缩放下不变。精确枚举显示 Wald 区间在均衡分配下方差估计有 1−1/n 向下偏差，几乎处处低于名义覆盖。

Conclusion: EO 区间提供了一个具有覆盖保证、闭式可解且界可允许的比例差置信区间，取代了几乎处处覆盖不足的 Wald 区间；代价是极端比例情形下的可精确量化的过度覆盖。

Abstract: Constructing a confidence interval for the difference between two independent binomial proportions involves a nuisance direction that is not identified by the estimand. The one-sample Wilson score interval inverts a scalar score test, but has no direct bivariate analogue isolating the difference: inverting the joint normal approximation yields an elliptical region in the unit square, whereas the estimand \(p_1-p_2\) is one-dimensional. We define the Elliptically Optimal (EO) confidence interval as the range of \(p_1-p_2\) over this region and solve the resulting optimization problem in closed form, obtaining explicit bounds in six mutually exclusive and exhaustive cases. The solution admits a compact characterization: the EO interval is the score interval obtained by maximizing over the nuisance variance rather than estimating it. It is therefore the shortest interval obtained by projecting the elliptical region, and inherits its coverage guarantee. We derive the exact coverage excess, \(2[Φ(z\mathcal{R})-Φ(z)]\), where \(\mathcal{R}\) is the ratio of the least-favourable to the true standard deviation. The excess vanishes on an explicit line through the parameter space, is bounded by \(α\), and is invariant under proportional scaling of the sample sizes. Exact enumeration of the binomial coverage shows that the Wald interval, whose variance estimator is downward biased by a factor \(1-1/n\) under balanced allocation, falls below nominal coverage almost everywhere. The EO interval never under-covers under the normal approximation and always yields admissible, non-degenerate bounds. Its price is over-coverage when both proportions are extreme, which we quantify exactly.

### [2] [Weighted Empirical Risk Minimization for Machine Learning under Long-Range Dependence: Exact Pathwise Rates and Learning-Error Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10767)
*Elina Moldavskaya*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文为长记忆数据上正则加权经验风险最小化训练的平滑参数模型建立了精确的几乎必然学习理论，给出了几乎必然Bahadur表示、参数学习的精确limsup律及完整学习轨迹的聚簇集合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 长程依赖数据下的学习理论多关注收敛速率，缺乏对几乎必然路径行为的精确刻画；加权经验风险最小化在长记忆情形下的极限律、常数与轨迹几何尚不明确。

Method: 设训练观测来自平稳高斯序列的固定有限窗口，样本权重正则变化。假设总体极小点处损失梯度的Wiener混沌秩为m且低频系数非零，在长记忆内部区间将有限滞后得分在迭代对数尺度上约化为单一加权Hermite混沌。

Result: 得到几乎必然Bahadur表示、学习参数的精确limsup律；当m≥2时得到完整学习轨迹的函数型聚簇集合。多项式学习指数由记忆参数与混沌秩决定，且在容许幂加权下不变；精确路径常数与聚簇几何依赖权重。秩一情形下，对容许幂指数做全局优化表明所有最优者均为正。

Conclusion: 该理论统一刻画了长记忆加权学习的几乎必然极限行为，区分了不变的学习指数与依赖权重的精细常数；时间序列预测与分类示例验证了结果。

Abstract: We develop an exact almost-sure learning theory for smooth parametric models trained by regularly weighted empirical risk minimization on long-range dependent data. The training observations are generated from a fixed finite window of a stationary Gaussian sequence, and the sample weights are regularly varying. If the loss gradient at the population minimizer has Wiener-chaos rank $m$ and a nonzero low-frequency coefficient, then, in the long-memory interior regime, the finite-lag score reduces on the iterated-logarithm scale to a single weighted Hermite chaos. This yields an almost-sure Bahadur representation, an exact limsup law for the learned parameter, and, for $m\ge2$, the functional cluster set of the complete learning trajectory. The polynomial learning exponent is determined by the memory parameter and the chaos rank and is invariant under the admissible power weighting, whereas the sharp pathwise constant and cluster geometry depend on the weights. In the rank-one case, global optimization over the admissible power exponents shows that every optimizer is positive. Time-series prediction and classification examples illustrate the results.

</details>


### [3] [Near-Optimal Reinforcement Learning with Multi-Step Transition Lookahead](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11807)
*Corentin Pla,Hugo Richard,Marc Abeille,Vianney Perchet*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文研究带转移前瞻的强化学习：证明对任意固定有理折扣因子，精确规划仍是NP难的，同时给出对每个固定前瞻深度的随机多项式时间近似方案，并扩展至未知转移与随机奖励，达到与经典表格折扣RL匹配的累积遗憾。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 带多步转移前瞻的最优规划虽能显著提升性能，但已知是NP难的——然而该硬度结果仅用趋于1的折扣因子证明，因此对任意固定折扣因子是否仍难、以及能否高效近似规划尚不清楚。

Method: 首先对每个固定有理折扣因子γ∈(0,1)证明精确规划NP难；其次提出对每个固定前瞻深度的随机多项式时间近似方案（FPRAS式算法）；再通过乐观策略与方差自适应置信界将方法扩展到未知转移和随机奖励场景。

Result: 证明了任意固定有理折扣因子下精确规划仍NP难；所提近似方案可高效实现近最优规划；扩展算法在未知转移与随机奖励下取得累积遗憾，其主导项与经典表格折扣RL匹配（至多对数因子）。

Conclusion: 尽管带转移前瞻的精确规划是NP难的，但高效的近最优规划与学习仍然可行，从而厘清了该问题的计算复杂性与可学习性边界。

Abstract: We study reinforcement learning (RL) with transition look-ahead, where the agent may observe which states would be visited upon playing any sequence of $\ell$ actions before deciding its course of action. Although look-ahead can substantially improve achievable performance, it is known that optimal planning with multi-step transition look-ahead is NP-hard, but this hardness was established using discount factors arbitrarily close to one. It was therefore unknown whether the problem remains hard for any discount factor, and whether near-optimal planning can nevertheless be performed efficiently. We resolve both questions. First, we show that for every fixed rational discount factor ($γ\in(0,1)$), exact planning remains NP-hard. Second, we introduce a randomized polynomial-time approximation scheme for every fixed look-ahead depth. We then extend our approach to unknown transitions and stochastic rewards using optimism and variance-adaptive confidence bounds. The resulting algorithm achieves cumulative regret whose leading term matches classical tabular discounted RL up to logarithmic factors. Thus, although exact planning with transition look-ahead is NP-hard, efficient near-optimal planning and learning remain possible.

</details>

### [4] [Generalized Ridge Refitting for the Lasso and Prediction Improvement Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10933)
*Guo Liu*

Main category: stat.ME

TL;DR: 研究Lasso等相集上二次修正估计量的统一框架，给出闭式表示及有限样本预测改进期望界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Lasso存在收缩偏差，二次修正（如Lasso-Ridge、重拟合）被广泛用作补救，但缺乏解释何时能改善预测的统一理论，需刻画选择随机性的影响。

Method: 在Lasso等相集上施加二次修正，惩罚矩阵决定修正幅度与几何；推导闭式表示并分离正的增益成分；将随机符号等相模型在确定性参考支持集附近局部化，以期望控制剩余随机线性项。

Result: 得到显式考虑Lasso模型选择随机性的有限样本期望界，并分解为增益项与随机项；框架涵盖各向同性Lasso-Ridge、最小二乘重拟合、Gram比例插值及坐标特定惩罚等情形。

Conclusion: 该分解为理解Lasso类二次修正何时能改进预测提供了统一框架。

Abstract: We study a class of Lasso based estimators obtained by applying a quadratic correction on the Lasso equicorrelation set. The penalty matrix determines both the magnitude and geometry of the correction and contains, among other cases, the isotropic Lasso--Ridge correction, least squares refitting, Gram proportional interpolation between the Lasso and least squares, and coordinate specific penalties. We first derive a closed form representation and isolate the positive gain component of the resulting prediction improvement. We then control the remaining stochastic linear term in expectation by localizing the random signed equicorrelation model around a deterministic reference support. This yields a finite sample expectation bound that explicitly accounts for the randomness induced by Lasso model selection. The resulting decomposition provides a unified framework for understanding when Lasso based quadratic corrections can improve prediction.

### [5] [Geospatial Foundation Models Capture Health-Relevant Dimensions of Place Beyond Conventional Social Risk Indices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11689)
*Nathaniel Hendrix,Carl Y. Zhang,Chris Heitzig,Andrew Bazemore,David H. Rehkopf*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 卫星影像驱动的四个地理空间基础模型生成的数值表征，能解释传统社会风险指数无法涵盖的社区健康差异，可作为其补充。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于区域的社会风险指数（ADI、SDI、SVI）概括了居民社会经济状况，但未充分捕捉可能影响健康的物理环境特征，导致残差方差未被解释。

Method: 利用2022年卫星数据，由四个地理空间基础模型家族生成物理场所的数值表征；用LightGBM预测美国本土82,646个普查 tract 的ACS变量及40项CDC PLACES慢性病与健康行为结局，并在10个留出州上评估性能。

Result: 对调查变量预测中等（如住房类型R²达0.54），但对残疾、失业、收入差距较弱；对健康结局可解释社会风险指数未解释方差的至多54%，在年度体检、关节炎、高血压上增益最大；40项结局的平均总解释方差由最小tract规模的0.31升至最大的0.39。

Conclusion: 地理空间基础模型能捕捉传统社会风险指数未涵盖的健康相关场所特征，可有效增强流行病学分析。

Abstract: Area-based social risk indices summarize residents' socioeconomic conditions but incompletely capture physical features of place that may affect health. We evaluated whether numerical representations of physical place produced by four geospatial foundation model families from 2022 satellite data explained residual variance in tract-level associations between the Area Deprivation Index, Social Deprivation Index, and Social Vulnerability Index with health outcomes. We used LightGBM to predict variables from the American Community Survey and 40 chronic disease and health-behavior outcomes from CDC PLACES across 82,646 census tracts in the contiguous United States, evaluating performance across 10 held-out states. Among survey variables, models were moderately predictive of some variables including housing type (R-squared up to 0.54) but weak for disability, unemployment, and income disparity. For health outcomes, models explained up to 54% of variance left unexplained by social risk indices, with the largest gains for annual checkups, arthritis, and high blood pressure. Mean total variance explained by geospatial foundation models across the 40 health-related outcomes increased from 0.31 in the smallest tract-size decile to 0.39 in the largest. Geospatial foundation models capture health-relevant features of place not represented by conventional social risk indices and may usefully augment them in epidemiological analyses.

</details>
