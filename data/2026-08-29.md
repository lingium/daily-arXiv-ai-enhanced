### [1] [Beyond Participant-Level Cross-Validation: Reliable Inference for Longitudinal Machine Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26205)
*Shahran Rahman Alve*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 本文针对纵向感知研究中参与者数量少但记录海量导致的伪重复（pseudo-replication）问题，提出了一种参与者级别的分析匹配置换检验（analysis-matched permutation test），通过在置换参与者标签后重跑整个标签依赖的流程（特征构建、预处理、调参、校准、候选选择）来获得正确的零分布。模拟显示该方法在20-80名参与者的队列中将I类错误控制在0.025-0.100，而窗口级推断在无效应时拒绝率高达70-80%。实际应用中，腕部活动数据（n=55）AUROC为0.928（p=0.0050），而智能手机感知（n=38）AUROC为0.636（p=0.1724）不能拒绝零假设。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 纵向感知研究通常从几十名参与者中收集数千个窗口数据，记录虽多但独立科学单元很少。当结果在每个参与者层面上定义时，这种不匹配使看似精确的发现容易受到伪重复、数据划分选择和多种分析管线灵活比较的影响。现有的参与者分割方法只能防止同一参与者的记录横跨训练测试分割，但无法校准产生最终报告数字的整个标签依赖流程（包括折构建、预处理、调参、校准和候选选择）。

Method: 作者定义了参与者级别的目标量（estimand），并提出分析匹配的零假设检验：通过置换参与者标签并重跑整个标签依赖的工作流程来获得与分析方法匹配的零分布。与冻结已选管线（freezing）相比，重复搜索（repeating the search）能更准确地控制错误率。核心原则是：每个读取标签的步骤都必须包含在置换分析内部。

Result: 在受控模拟中：窗口级推断在无效应时70-80%的重复中拒绝，窗口自助法以相同比率拒绝；参与者自助法仍以10-17%拒绝；分析匹配检验在20-80名参与者的队列中保持0.025-0.100的错误率。冻结已选管线而非重复搜索会将I类错误从0.040膨胀至0.240（8个候选时）。实际应用中：腕部活动追踪（n=55）得到参与者AUROC 0.928，p=0.0050，结论在多种稳健性分析下保持一致；智能手机感知（n=38，7个阳性）AUROC为0.636，p=0.1724不能拒绝零假设（敏感性0.143）。

Conclusion: 实际规则很明确：所有读取标签的步骤都必须放在置换分析内部，重复记录不会创造额外的独立参与者。分析匹配的置换检验是控制纵向感知研究伪重复问题的有效方法，它能够正确校准整个分析工作流的不确定性，防止因样本量虚高而得出误导性的显著结论。

Abstract: Longitudinal sensing studies routinely collect thousands of windows from a few dozen participants. The records are numerous; the independent scientific units are not. When the outcome is defined per participant, this mismatch makes apparently precise findings vulnerable to pseudo-replication, to partition choice, and to the ordinary analytic flexibility of comparing several pipelines before reporting one. Splitting on participants prevents a person's records from straddling a split, but it does not calibrate the label-dependent workflow fold construction, preprocessing, tuning, calibration, and candidate selection that produced the reported number. We define a participant-level estimand and obtain an analysis-matched null by permuting participant labels and rerunning that entire workflow. In controlled simulation, window-level inference rejects in 70-80% of replicates when no effect exists and a window bootstrap rejects at the same rate; a participant bootstrap still rejects at 10-17%; the analysis-matched test holds 0.025-0.100 across cohorts of 20 to 80 participants. Freezing the selected pipeline instead of repeating the search inflates Type-I error to 0.240 with eight candidates, where repeating it holds 0.040. Applied to two public cohorts, wrist actigraphy (n=55) yields participant AUROC 0.928 with p=0.0050, a conclusion that persists under a scale-robust rank-pooled statistic and under a matched permutation null computed after excluding hospitalized participants (p=0.0089). Smartphone sensing (n=38, 7 positives) yields 0.636 and does not reject (p=0.1724) despite sufficient resolution, with sensitivity 0.143. The practical rule is narrow: every step that reads labels belongs inside the permuted analysis, and repeated records do not create additional independent participants.

### [2] [A causal graph-informed temporal convolution architecture for interpretable retail electricity price forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26234)
*Yufan Ji,Abdollah Shafieezadeh,Noah Dormady*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 本研究提出CG-TCN（因果图信息时间卷积网络），将学习到的因果图通过图神经嵌入集成到时间卷积网络中，用于增强零售电价预测的准确性和可解释性。该模型在俄亥俄州解除管制市场的十年日度数据上，对1、10、15步前瞻预测分别达到3.08%、3.82%和5.43%的MAPE，优于基准模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解除管制的零售电力市场面临显著的价格波动以及与远期/期货产品的复杂交互，给运营决策带来挑战。现有预测方法难以同时兼顾预测精度和对价格动态机制的理解，因此需要一种既能提高预测准确率又能提供可解释洞察的架构。

Method: CG-TCN采用三步流程：(1) 多分辨率分解，将半年度、季度和月度趋势与高频波动分离；(2) 在分解成分和关键协变量（批发远期价格、零售合同属性如提前终止费）上，利用领域约束（保持因果方向性和外生性）发现因果图；(3) 将学习到的因果结构编码为邻接嵌入，用于调节TCN的卷积和注意力机制，使表征学习与因果路径对齐。

Result: 使用俄亥俄州解除管制市场十年日度12个月固定价格住宅合同数据，CG-TCN在1、10、15步前瞻预测中MAPE分别为3.08%、3.82%和5.43%，一致优于基准模型。研究发现批发远期价格主要决定长期零售价格趋势，而合同属性影响短期波动。

Conclusion: CG-TCN将预测性能与可解释性结合，为竞争性电力市场提供透明且与政策相关的洞察，可支持市场分析、消费者保护、监管监督、风险评估和采购规划。

Abstract: Retail electricity markets in deregulated systems face significant price volatility and complex interactions with forward and futures products, posing challenges for effective operational decision-making. This study introduces a Causal Graph-Informed Temporal Convolutional Network (CG-TCN), a forecasting architecture that integrates a learned causal graph into a temporal convolutional network via a graph-neural embedding to enhance both forecasting accuracy and interpretability of retail electricity price dynamics. It first applies a multi-resolution decomposition to isolate semiannual, quarterly, and monthly trends from high-frequency fluctuations. A causal graph is then discovered over these components and key covariates, including wholesale forward prices and retail contract attributes such as early termination fees, with domain constraints that preserve causal directionality and exogeneity. The learned causal structure is encoded as an adjacency embedding that conditions the TCN's convolutions and attention, aligning representation learning with causal pathways. Using ten years of daily 12-month fixed-price residential contracts from Ohio's deregulated market, we find that wholesale forward prices primarily determine long-term retail price trends, whereas contract attributes influence short-term fluctuations. CG-TCN consistently outperforms benchmark models, achieving mean absolute percentage errors of 3.08%, 3.82%, and 5.43% for one-, ten-, and fifteen-step-ahead forecasts of daily retail electricity median prices, respectively. By combining predictive performance with interpretability, CG-TCN provides transparent, policy-relevant insight to support market analytics, consumer protection, regulatory oversight, risk assessment and procurement planning in competitive electricity markets.

### [3] [Copula based dependent censoring in cure models with covariates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26212)
*Morine Delhelle,Anouar El Ghouch,Ingrid Van Keilegom*

Main category: stat.ME

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种可处理依赖删失、独立管理删失和治愈分数的全参数混合治愈模型扩展，通过copula建模生存时间与删失时间的相依性，并允许协变量影响所有模型参数，从而实现更个体化的估计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生存分析中时间-事件变量常受右删失影响，现有模型要么未区分依赖删失与独立删失，要么缺乏治愈分数和协变量效应。研究者希望更准确地建模生存与删失时间之间的依赖关系，并增强模型灵活性。

Method: 在已有的基于copula的全参数混合治愈模型基础上进行扩展，纳入独立管理删失时间A，并允许协变量影响所有模型参数（包括治愈分数、依赖结构等），从而提供更个体化的估计并放宽识别条件。

Result: 该扩展框架能够更准确地建模生存与删失时间之间的依赖关系，通过协变量效应提供更大的灵活性，实现治愈分数、依赖结构及其他临床相关量的个体化估计。

Conclusion: 协变量的引入不仅提升了模型的灵活性和个性化估计能力，还使得模型的识别条件得以放宽，从而在实际应用中具有更广泛的适用性。

Abstract: In survival analysis, the time-to-event variable T is frequently subject to right censoring. Individuals may withdraw from the study for various reasons, or may not experience the event of interest before the end of follow-up. In this paper, we distinguish between two types of censoring: a potentially dependent censoring time C, which may be stochastically related to T, and an independent administrative censoring time A. In addition, the data may exhibit a cure fraction, meaning that some individuals will never experience the event. We build upon a recent work about a fully parametric mixture cure model, which accounts for dependent censoring through copulas. The proposed extension incorporates administrative censoring and allows covariates to affect all model parameters. This framework enables a more accurate modelling of the dependence between survival and censoring times while providing greater flexibility through covariate effects, leading to more individualised estimation of the cure fraction, the dependence structure, and other clinically relevant quantities. Moreover, the presence of covariates allows for weaker identification conditions.
