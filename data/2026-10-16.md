Result: 在所有区域和所有考虑的预热期(lead times)内，AIWP模型均明显优于ECMWF IFS集合预报，包括对破纪录事件；AIWP更好地预报极端事件的发生与强度，尾部校准更优，遗漏的纪录更少，站点观测也证实了这一结果。

Conclusion: 许多已报道的AIWP局限性源于不合适的验证选择，特别是比较了目标量不同的确定性预报；对极端事件的公平评估需要采用概率性验证。

Abstract: AI weather prediction (AIWP) models have emerged as a key complement to physics-based numerical weather prediction (NWP) models, and now often outperform them on aggregate skill. Yet, several studies report that physics-based models still forecast extreme events better, in particular record-breaking extremes, based on the evaluation of deterministic forecasts. Here we evaluate heat extreme forecasts from probabilistic AIWP models over global land and show that they clearly outperform the ECMWF IFS ensemble, both raw and post-processed, including for record-breaking events, in all regions and at all lead times considered. AIWP models better forecast the occurrence and intensity of extremes, are better calibrated in the tail and miss fewer records. Station observations confirm these results. We argue that many reported AIWP limitations stem from unsuitable verification choices, specifically comparing deterministic forecasts that target different quantities. Instead, a fair evaluation of extremes necessitates probabilistic verification.
