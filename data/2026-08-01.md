### [1] [Expected Survival-Time Bounds for Robust Optimization Over Time under Isotropic Gaussian Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27280)
*Pavel Novoa-Hernández*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文研究了鲁棒优化随时间演化（ROOT）问题中固定部署解的期望生存时间，在各项同性高斯环境动态下，将生存建模为离散首逃问题，推导出严格下界和可计算的多步上界。分析表明在缓慢变化环境中期望生存时间以Θ(σ⁻²)规模增长，在高维环境下接近最小值1。蒙特卡洛实验验证了理论预测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ROOT领域虽有大量算法和实证研究，但生存时间这一核心属性的理论基础极为薄弱。生存时间虽被广泛用作时间鲁棒性度量，但其期望值与环境动态、部署质量及问题特征之间的依赖关系几乎未被理解。本文旨在填补这一理论空白。

Method: 将固定部署解在各项同性高斯环境动态下的生存建模为离散首逃问题（discrete first-exit problem），推导期望生存时间的严格下界和可计算的多步上界，并通过蒙特卡洛模拟进行验证和灵敏度分析。

Result: 在缓慢变化环境中期望生存时间以Θ(σ⁻²)的阶数扩展，在高维环境中趋于最小值1（即仅能维持到下一次环境变化）。蒙特卡洛实验验证了理论预测，并考察了模型假设与参数不确定性的影响。

Conclusion: 该框架提供了部署寿命的解析刻画，能够判断所需部署时间窗在何种条件下可以保证、可以排除、或无法解析解决，为优化后的部署决策提供了理论支撑。

Abstract: Robust Optimization Over Time (ROOT) is a recent branch of evolutionary dynamic optimization that seeks solutions capable of remaining effective across multiple consecutive environments. Unlike the traditional track-the-moving-optimum (TMO) paradigm, which reoptimizes after every environmental change, ROOT explicitly values persistence. Although the field has grown considerably, most contributions remain algorithmic and empirical, leaving several fundamental properties poorly understood from a theoretical perspective. One such property is survival time, defined as the number of future environments in which a deployed solution continues to satisfy a prescribed quality threshold. While survival time is widely used as a measure of temporal robustness, little is known about how its expected value depends on environmental dynamics, deployment quality, or problem characteristics. This paper studies expected survival time for a fixed deployed solution under isotropic Gaussian environmental dynamics. Modeling survival as a discrete first-exit problem, we derive a rigorous lower bound and a computable multi-step upper bound. The analysis shows that expected survival scales as $Θ(σ^-{2})$ in slowly varying environments and approaches its minimum value of one future change in high dimensions. A comprehensive Monte Carlo study validates the theoretical predictions, examines sensitivity to modeling assumptions and parameter uncertainty, and illustrates how the bounds can support deployment decisions after optimization. The resulting framework provides an analytical characterization of deployment lifetime and identifies when a required deployment horizon can be guaranteed, ruled out, or remains analytically unresolved.

### [2] [An analysis of binary isotonic regression: degrees of freedom and implications for calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27301)
*Raphael Rossellini,Rina Foygel Barber,Zhimei Ren,Jake A. Soloff*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文对二元样本上保序回归的最坏情况自由度给出了精确的有限样本刻画，得到了领先项为 (3/(4π²)^(1/3)) n^(2/3) 的尖锐界；并以此为基础首次推导出保序回归的期望校准误差（ECE）的非平凡分布无关保证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 保序回归是估计单调函数和校准概率预测器的经典工具，但对其自由度（衡量模型复杂度）的有限样本最坏情况刻画此前不够精确；同时，校准是概率预测的核心要求，而保序回归作为常用的校准后处理方法，缺乏分布无关的期望校准误差保证。

Method: 利用解析数论方法，识别二元序列中使保序回归产生最多不同拟合值的序列，从而精确刻画最坏情况自由度；在此基础上，利用确定性的自由度界推导保序回归的期望校准误差（ECE）的上界。

Result: 得到了自由度的一个尖锐界，其领先项为 (3/(4π²)^(1/3)) n^(2/3)，改进了此前的界；并首次获得了仅假设 Y∈{0,1} 的、完全模型无关且分布无关的保序回归 ECE 保证。

Conclusion: 本文在理论上完善了保序回归自由度的有限样本分析，并首次为其校准性能提供了非平凡的分布无关理论保证，推进了对保序回归作为校准工具的理解。

Abstract: Isotonic regression is a canonical tool for estimating monotone functions and calibrating probabilistic predictors. We provide a fully sharp finite-sample characterization of its worst-case degrees of freedom on binary samples. Specifically, we identify the binary sequences that maximize the number of distinct fitted values produced by isotonic regression. We develop a sharp bound on the degrees of freedom with a leading term of $\frac{3}{(4π^2)^{1/3}} n^{2/3}$ using analytic number theory, improving on previous bounds.
  We then apply this result to calibration. Calibration is a central requirement for probabilistic prediction, and isotonic regression is a widely used post-processing method for improving calibration. Building on deterministic degrees-of-freedom bounds, we derive, to our knowledge, the first nontrivial distribution-free guarantee on the Expected Calibration Error (ECE) of isotonic regression. This ECE bound is fully model-free and distribution-free, only assuming $Y \in \{0,1\}$.

### [3] [Robust Wavelength Selection for Partial Least Squares Sugar Content Estimation Using Combinatorial Bayesian Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27645)
*Mitsunobu Kanebako,Ami S. Koshikawa,Masaru Hitomi,Takuro Tanaka,Mahito Chiba,Maiko Mori,Masayuki Ohzeki*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于贝叶斯优化的波长区域选择方法，将近红外光谱中糖含量估计的波长选择问题形式化为二元黑盒优化问题，通过稀疏二次代理模型和Thompson采样结合模拟退火/量子退火求解QUBO问题，实验表明该方法优于遗传算法和模拟退火，能选出更稳定、更一致的波长区域。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近红外光谱中波长选择是重要的预处理步骤，可提高预测精度和可解释性。传统方法（如遗传算法）可能存在不稳定或陷入过拟合的问题，因此需要更鲁棒的特征选择方法。

Method: 将波长区域选择建模为二元黑盒优化问题；构建稀疏二次代理模型；通过Thompson采样逐步提取感兴趣的波长区域；将最小化采集函数转化为QUBO问题，利用模拟退火或量子退火求解。

Result: 该方法提高了偏最小二乘回归的预测精度；相比遗传算法和模拟退火，选出的波长区域更一致；在一位局部扰动下，验证集RMSE波动极小，表明收敛到更平滑的误差景观，避免孤立过拟合解。

Conclusion: 组合贝叶斯优化是光谱预测任务中鲁棒特征选择的有效框架。

Abstract: Wavelength selection is one of the important preprocessing methods in near-infrared spectroscopy to improve prediction accuracy and interpretability of spectral data. We formulate wavelength-region selection for sugar content estimation as a binary black-box optimization problem and propose a method based on Bayesian optimization. The proposed method constructs a sparse quadratic surrogate model and sequentially extracts interested wavelength regions by Thompson sampling. Minimizing an acquisition function is performed as a quadratic unconstrained binary optimization problem by simulated or quantum annealing. Experiments show that the proposed method improves the prediction accuracy of partial least squares regression and yields more consistent wavelength regions than genetic-algorithm-based selection and simulated annealing. Under one-bit local perturbations, the selected wavelength regions show minimal fluctuations in root mean square errors between observed and predicted values of a validation set. This local stability suggests that our method converges to a smoother error landscape and avoids isolated overfitted solutions. These results indicate that combinatorial Bayesian optimization is a useful framework for robust feature selection in spectroscopic prediction tasks.

### [4] [Generalization and Trade-off in Adversarial Training: An RKHS Perspective via Kernel Integral Operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27995)
*Yiling Xie,Xiaoming Huo*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文在再生核希尔伯特空间（RKHS）框架下研究对抗训练。作者推导了基于核积分算子的源均匀泛化误差界，并证明在多项式谱模型下，对抗训练的均衡泛化率可能慢于极小极大预测基准，揭示了对抗训练中统计精度的损失。该损失源于对抗鲁棒性与观测噪声的交互作用。为此，作者提出一种两阶段噪声去偏方法，通过估计并移除混合项中的噪声贡献，使估计器在样本相关鲁棒性水平下达到（除对数因子外）极小极大多项式速率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对抗训练是保护模型免受对抗攻击的有效方法，但其在非参数框架下的泛化行为尚未被充分理解。作者希望从RKHS和核积分算子的角度，深入刻画对抗训练在鲁棒性与统计精度之间的权衡，解决对抗训练可能存在的统计精度损失问题。

Method: 1) 在RKHS框架下，通过核积分算子推导对抗训练估计器的源均匀泛化误差界；2) 在多项式谱模型下建立匹配的下界，证明最优均衡泛化率慢于极小极大基准；3) 分析该损失源于鲁棒性与噪声的交互作用（混合鲁棒项中的噪声贡献减慢逼近速率）；4) 提出两阶段噪声去偏方法，先估计再移除噪声贡献，使估计器达到极小极大多项式速率。

Result: 推导了RKHS对抗训练估计器的泛化误差界；证明了对抗训练相对于标准预测存在统计精度损失；提出的两阶段噪声去偏方法在合适的鲁棒性水平下达到（对数因子内）极小极大多项式速率；数值实验验证了理论发现和方法有效性。

Conclusion: 本文系统刻画了非参数框架下对抗训练的泛化行为，揭示了对抗鲁棒性与统计精度权衡的新解释：噪声在混合鲁棒项中的作用是减慢逼近速率但降低估计复杂度。基于此理解提出的噪声去偏方法为缓解对抗训练的精度损失提供了原则性解决方案。

Abstract: Adversarial training has emerged as a powerful approach for protecting models against adversarial attacks in a broad range of real-world applications. In this paper, we study adversarial training in the reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) framework through the associated kernel integral operator. We first derive source-uniform generalization error bounds for the RKHS adversarial training estimator in terms of the robustness level, sample size, source smoothness, and kernel spectrum. On a fixed polynomial-spectrum model, we further establish a matching lower bound showing that the optimally balanced generalization rate can be slower than the minimax prediction benchmark. This result reveals a loss of statistical accuracy in adversarial training. Our analysis shows that this loss arises from the interaction between adversarial robustness and observation noise: the noise contribution in the mixed robustness term slows the approximation rate, although the same term reduces the estimation complexity. To address this limitation, we propose a two-stage noise-debiased procedure that estimates and removes the noise contribution from the mixed term. The resulting estimator improves the generalization rate and attains the minimax polynomial rate, up to a logarithmic factor, when the robustness level is selected at the stated sample-dependent order. Our results characterize the generalization behavior of adversarial training in a nonparametric framework and provide a new interpretation and a principled solution for the trade-off between adversarial robustness and generalization. Numerical experiments support the theoretical findings and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

</details>
