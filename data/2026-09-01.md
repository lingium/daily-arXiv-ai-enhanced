### [1] [Learning between the peaks: sharp asymptotics for kernel ridge regression under power-law anisotropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28564)
*Lorenzo Rizzi,Arie Wortsman Zurich,Bruno Loureiro*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文研究了各向异性高斯数据下核岭回归问题，在多项式内积核与输入协方差幂律衰减（指数α≥0）的设定下，推导了核谱与泛化误差在多项式高维区域（n=Θ(d^κ)）的渐近精确表达式。研究发现：弱各向异性（0<α<1）下问题仍保持高维特征但峰值被抑制；强各向异性（α>1）下有效维度变为常数，方差不再依赖样本量。结果揭示了输入几何如何塑造核特征并从根本上影响泛化性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的核方法理论分析大多假设各向同性数据，而真实数据往往具有各向异性结构（协方差矩阵按幂律衰减）。理解输入几何（各向异性）如何影响核谱和泛化误差是核方法理论的重要开放问题，本文旨在系统填补这一空白。

Method: 在多项式高维区域n=Θ(d^κ)设定下，为各向异性高斯输入与多项式内积核推导核谱和泛化误差的渐近精确表达式。通过分析α（协方差衰减指数）的不同区间，分别刻画弱各向异性（0<α<1）和强各向异性（α>1）下的行为，并将结果特化到单指标目标以展示索引与主方向对齐的影响。

Result: (1) 弱各向异性下：方差仍在整数样本复杂度处出现峰值，但随α增大而逐渐衰减；与主方向强对齐的目标在分数样本复杂度处出现偏差下降，使偏差转变与插值峰值解耦。(2) 强各向异性下：有效维度恒定，方差在无脊插值下进入平台期，在固定脊惩罚下以显式速率消失；偏差转变由目标衰减速率决定——低于阈值时学习是突变的，高于阈值时以恢复经典source和capacity速率的幂律衰减。(3) 单指标目标的特化结果表明索引与数据主方向的对齐决定了各向异性对学习的影响。

Conclusion: 本文系统揭示了输入协方差各向异性如何重塑核谱与泛化曲线：弱各向异性保留部分各向同性高维特征但抑制插值峰值并使偏差转变与插值解耦；强各向异性则使问题有效维度恒定，实现剧烈且截然不同的学习行为。这些发现深化了对输入几何在核方法泛化性质中根本作用的理際。

Abstract: We study kernel ridge regression under anisotropic Gaussian data, where the input covariance decays as a power law with exponent $α\geq 0$ for polynomial inner-product kernels. We derive asymptotically sharp expressions for the kernel spectrum and the generalization error in the polynomial high-dimensional regime $n=Θ(d^κ)$, revealing how anisotropy reshapes the learning curves. For weak anisotropy ($0<α<1$), the problem remains effectively high-dimensional and retains some features of the isotropic case, while departing from it in others: the variance still peaks at integer sample complexities $κ\in\mathbb{N}$, but these peaks are progressively damped as $α$ grows; meanwhile, for targets strongly aligned with the data's principal directions, the bias drops at fractional sample complexities, decoupling the bias transitions from the interpolation peaks. For strong anisotropy ($α> 1$), the effective dimension of the problem is constant, and the variance stops depending on sample size altogether, plateauing under ridgeless interpolation or vanishing at an explicit rate under fixed ridge penalty. The bias undergoes a sharp transition governed by the target's decay rate: below a threshold, learning is abrupt rather than gradual; above it, the bias decays as a power law that recovers the classical source and capacity rates. We finally specialize these results to single-index targets, showing how the alignment of the index with the data's principal directions determines the effect of anisotropy on learning. Together, our results clarify how the input geometry shapes the kernel features and fundamentally impacts its generalization properties.

### [2] [A ridgeline correspondence criterion: the number of modes of a Gaussian mixture is finite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28558)
*Carlos Améndola,Jose Israel Rodriguez*

Main category: math.ST

TL;DR: 本文证明任意有限维多元高斯混合密度仅有有限多个模态（modes）。通过结合Ray和Lindsay（2005）的脊线理论的代数形式化，以及基于Ax函数超越定理的超越度论证，来界定临界点集合的基数。该技术扩展了Wang（2026）关于同方差高斯混合的工作，引入了"脊线对应"（ridgeline correspondence）概念，将有限性结果推广到任意异方差高斯混合，并进一步推广到多项式指数混合和广义高斯混合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高斯混合模型的模态数量问题在统计学中至关重要（如聚类分析、密度估计），但异方差高斯混合的模态有限性问题长期以来悬而未决。前期工作仅解决了同方差情形，本文旨在为更一般的异方差情形提供严格的数学证明。

Method: 将脊线理论代数化，结合Ax的函数超越定理进行超越度论证，引入"脊线对应"概念来统一处理同方差和异方差情形，从而界定临界点数量。

Result: 证明了任意有限多元高斯混合密度仅有有限多个模态；此外该框架还建立了多项式指数混合和广义高斯混合的模态有限性。

Conclusion: 该工作在理论上解决了高斯混合模态数有限性的开放问题，并为更广泛混合分布族的模态分析提供了统一框架。

Abstract: We prove that every finite multivariate Gaussian mixture density has only finitely many modes. Our approach combines an algebraic formulation of the ridgeline theory of Ray and Lindsay (2005) with a transcendence-degree argument based on Ax's functional-transcendence theorem to bound the cardinality of the set of critical points. Our techniques extend recent work by Wang (2026), who used Ax's theorem together with real-analytic curve selection to prove finiteness of the critical set of homoscedastic Gaussian mixtures. We introduce the ridgeline correspondence and use it to obtain a finiteness result that applies to arbitrary heteroscedastic Gaussian mixtures. Our framework also establishes finiteness of the number of modes for additional classes of polynomial-exponential mixtures and generalized Gaussian mixtures.

</details>

### [3] [Learning a Size-Weight Frontier for Synthetic-Augmented Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28576)
*Chengpiao Huang,Kaizheng Wang*

Main category: stat.ME

TL;DR: 本文提出一个通用框架，用于在多个相关任务群体中进行合成数据增强的统计推断。该框架通过"规模-权重前沿"（size-weight frontier）刻画合成增强，并基于历史任务估计该前沿，从而同时为所有位于前沿下方或之上的规模-权重配置提供有限样本覆盖保证。实验表明，在用大型语言模型响应增强民意调查数据时，该方法能达到目标覆盖并显著收窄置信区间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 真实数据稀缺时，合成数据可用于改善统计推断，但朴素地将合成样本视为真实数据会引入偏差并导致推断不可靠。因此需要一种通用的框架来指导合成数据的合理使用，避免偏差的同时提升推断效率。

Method: 提出了一个跨相关任务总体的合成增强推断通用框架，用合成观测数量及其权重来刻画合成增强。核心是一个"规模-权重前沿"：对每个权重，指定能达到目标任务边际覆盖的最大合成样本量。从历史任务估计该前沿，并建立对所有位于估计前沿下方或之上的规模-权重配置同时成立的有限样本覆盖保证。

Result: 在利用大语言模型响应增强民意调查数据的实验中，所提方法达到了目标覆盖水平，并大幅收窄了置信区间，验证了框架的有效性。

Conclusion: 该框架为合成数据的合理使用提供了理论保证，在确保覆盖可靠性的同时提升了推断精度，在数据稀缺场景下具有较强的实用价值。

Abstract: Synthetic data can improve statistical inference when real data are scarce, but naively treating synthetic samples as real data can introduce bias and lead to unreliable inference. We develop a general framework for synthetic-augmented inference across a population of related tasks. It characterizes synthetic augmentation by the number of synthetic observations and their weight. Central to our framework is a size-weight frontier that specifies, for each weight, the largest synthetic sample size for which all smaller sizes attain the target task-marginal coverage. We estimate this frontier from historical tasks, and establish a finite-sample coverage guarantee simultaneously for all size-weight configurations on or below the estimated frontier. In experiments using large language model responses to augment opinion survey data, our procedure achieves target coverage and substantially narrows confidence intervals.

</details>
