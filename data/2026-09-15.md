### [1] [A class of nonparametric homogeneity tests on the circle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13019)
*Alberto Fernández-de-Marcos,Eduardo García-Portugués*

Main category: math.ST

TL;DR: 提出圆上c样本同质性检验的统一框架，含Sobolev类检验及聚合框架，新增Anderson-Darling型检验与两个多峰检测检验，无需重采样。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 圆形数据上多样本同质性检验方法有限，缺乏统一框架和Anderson-Darling型、多峰检测等新型检验。

Method: 构建c样本Sobolev检验类，推广基于均匀得分的两样本Sobolev检验并涵盖已有方法；通过平均型、最大型、插值型等合并函数聚合样本分量；据此提出首个圆形Anderson-Darling型检验，以及基于softmax和Poisson核的多峰检验。

Result: 推导出检验类的渐近零分布，证明对广泛固定备择的一致性，得到移位型局部备择下的渐近分布；检验分布无关、无需重采样。模拟显示Anderson-Darling型检验在多场景下强于Cramér-von Mises型；softmax与Poisson检验在多种备择下有效；并应用于北极熊哺乳模式分析。

Conclusion: 该框架统一并扩展了圆形数据的c样本同质性检验，提供分布无关、无需重采样的有力工具，适用于实际数据分析。

Abstract: We develop a unified framework for $c$-sample homogeneity testing on the circle. The proposed class of $c$-sample Sobolev tests generalizes the two-sample Sobolev tests based on uniform scores and encompasses the existing multisample tests as particular cases. We further embed this class into a broader aggregation framework, where the samplewise Sobolev components are combined through general merging functions, including average-type, maximum-type, and interpolating families. Within this class, we introduce the first Anderson-Darling-type homogeneity test for circular data, and we further propose two new tests designed to detect multimodal departures from homogeneity, constructed from softmax and Poisson kernels. We derive the asymptotic null distribution of the class, prove its consistency against a broad family of fixed alternatives, and obtain the asymptotic distribution under shift-type local alternatives. The tests are distribution-free and therefore do not require resampling. A comprehensive simulation study demonstrates the strong power of the Anderson-Darling-type test compared with Cramér-von Mises-type competitors across several scenarios, and the effectiveness of the softmax and Poisson tests under several alternatives. The testing toolbox is applied to analyze the nursing patterns of polar bears.
