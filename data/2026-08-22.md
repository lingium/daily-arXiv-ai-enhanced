### [1] [Dummy RAPM:Representing Low-Minute Players in Regularized Adjusted Plus-Minus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19454)
*Kenny Watts,Jonathan Pipping-Gamón,Abraham J. Wyner*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 本文提出Dummy RAPM方法，通过为每侧被排除的低分钟球员数量添加五个虚拟变量，恢复RAPM中因移除低分钟球员列而丢失的阵容信息。在16个NBA赛季的验证中，该方法将持出赛月比赛的平均赛季比赛分差RMSE从12.897降至12.856，在13/16赛季中取得更低RMSE，平均降低0.042分（0.30%）。虽然预测精度提升幅度较小，但具有一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准RAPM在移除低分钟球员列后，其 stint 仍保留在数据中，但设计矩阵不再能完整代表实际阵容，导致信息丢失。作者希望通过添加虚拟变量来恢复这部分被排除球员数量的信息，从而改善RAPM的预测准确性。

Method: Dummy RAPM在标准RAPM基础上增加五个虚拟变量，分别表示每侧被排除球员的数量（0-4个）。通过16个NBA赛季的时序验证，确定每场出场10分钟为阈值，且虚拟变量与球员惩罚系数之比为2.2。使用持出（held-out）的3-4月比赛进行预测评估。

Result: Dummy RAPM将平均赛季比赛分差RMSE从12.897降低到12.856，在16个赛季中有13个赛季取得更低RMSE。平均降幅为0.042分（0.30%）。虽然绝对改进幅度小，但改进结果在大多数赛季中保持一致。

Conclusion: 当RAPM明确记录每侧被排除球员的数量时，其表现更优。这一发现表明，在球员贡献估计中，即使无法为低分钟球员单独建模，保留其存在信息（数量）仍有助于提高模型的预测准确性。

Abstract: Regularized Adjusted Plus-Minus (RAPM) uses stint-level lineup indicators to estimate player contributions to scoring margin. When low-minute player columns are removed, their stints remain in the data, but the design matrix no longer represents the complete lineup. Dummy RAPM restores this information using five indicators for the number of excluded players on each lineup side. Across 16 NBA seasons, chronological validation selects a 10-minute-per-appearance threshold and a dummy-to-player penalty ratio of 2.2. On held-out March-April games, Dummy RAPM reduces mean season game-margin RMSE from 12.897 to 12.856 and achieves lower RMSE in 13 of 16 seasons. The average reduction is 0.042 points, or 0.30%. Although the improvement in game-level predictive accuracy is small, it is consistent: RAPM performs better when it records how many excluded players are on each side.

### [2] [Discrete Diffusion Inference-Time Control with Nested Sequential Monte Carlo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20123)
*Lohithsai Yadala Chanchu,Hany Abdulsamad,Christian A. Naesseth*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 本文研究离散扩散语言模型中的推理时控制问题，提出使用嵌套顺序蒙特卡洛（NSMC）和全自适应嵌套SMC（FA-NSMC）方法，在无需重训练的情况下将采样引导至序列级奖励，并在毒性和流畅性控制任务上优于此前的最佳n采样和引导SMC方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的推理时控制方法存在缺陷：best-of-n采样可能过度乐观（overoptimism），而引导顺序蒙特卡洛（bootstrap SMC）存在权重退化（weight degeneracy）问题。作者旨在解决这些问题，提出更稳健的推理时控制方法。

Method: 将嵌套顺序蒙特卡洛（NSMC）和全自适应嵌套SMC（FA-NSMC）方法形式化应用于Feynman-Kac引导框架中，用于离散扩散语言模型的文本生成控制。作者还识别并纠正了先前NSMC公式中的偏差错误（biased final estimates）。

Result: 在毒性控制和流畅性控制任务上，NSMC和FA-NSMC方法始终优于best-of-n采样和引导SMC方法，表现出更好的控制效果。

Conclusion: 嵌套SMC方法为离散扩散语言模型的推理时控制提供了更有效且无偏的解决方案，克服了既有粒子方法的局限，为进一步研究序列级奖励引导的文本生成提供了新方向。

Abstract: We study inference-time control for text generation in discrete diffusion language models, where the goal is to steer sampling toward sequence-level rewards without retraining. Prior work in this domain has focused on particle-based methods such as best-of-$n$ sampling and bootstrap sequential Monte Carlo, which may suffer from overoptimism and weight degeneracy, respectively. We address these limitations using \emph{nested} sequential Monte Carlo methods. We formulate nested SMC (NSMC) and fully-adapted nested SMC (FA-NSMC) for Feynman--Kac steering, identifying and correcting errors in prior formulations that lead to biased final estimates. We evaluate these methods on toxicity and fluency steering tasks, showing that NSMC and FA-NSMC consistently outperform best-of-$n$ and bootstrap SMC.
